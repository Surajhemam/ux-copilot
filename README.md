# UX Copilot

**AI suggests. Designer decides.**

UX Copilot is an AI-assisted UX research workspace concept designed to help UX and product designers turn scattered research into structured insights, evidence-backed opportunities, and actionable user flows.

## The problem

UX research often involves large amounts of qualitative feedback, disconnected evidence, and repeated manual synthesis. Connecting research findings to meaningful design opportunities can be time-consuming and difficult to trace.

## The solution

UX Copilot brings the research synthesis workflow into one workspace, helping designers move from raw research material to actionable UX decisions while keeping human judgment at the center.

## Core experience

- **Overview:** Monitor projects and research activities.
- **Research:** Organize research sources and supporting material.
- **Insights:** Review, edit, accept, or reject synthesized insights.
- **Evidence:** Trace insights back to supporting research evidence.
- **Opportunities:** Translate validated findings into UX improvements.
- **User Flows:** Explore and refine potential user journeys.

## Design principle

**AI suggests. Designer decides.**

The concept emphasizes human oversight, evidence traceability, transparent AI-assisted suggestions, and designer control over decisions.

## Prototype scope

UX Copilot is a high-fidelity interactive prototype created to explore AI-assisted UX research workflows, interface design, interaction patterns, and design-system consistency.

AI-generated content and application data may be simulated. The prototype should not be interpreted as a fully production-ready AI service unless those capabilities are independently implemented and verified.

## What this project explores

- Human-AI interaction design
- UX research synthesis workflows
- Evidence-backed decision-making
- Information architecture and dashboard design
- Reusable UI components and design systems
- Interactive prototyping
